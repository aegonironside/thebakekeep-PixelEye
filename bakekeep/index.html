The real questions

The things people ask before they order.

Will it actually look like the photo I send?

Usually very close, and where it cannot be, you will be told before you pay. Hand piping is not printing, so small things differ. If a design is beyond what a home kitchen can do, that gets said honestly instead of guessed at.

Do I really have to pay before I see it?

Half, and only to hold the date. Ingredients get bought and the day gets blocked off for you. The other half is paid when the cake is in your hands.

How much is a cake?

It depends on size, design and flavour, so every cake is quoted. Send the details and a full price comes back before anything is mixed.

How early do I need to order?

Three days at the very least. Detailed designs want a week, and dates around holidays go early.

Do you deliver to me?

Divulapitiya, Minuwangoda, Meerigama, Negombo, Ja-Ela and Gampaha. Delivery is charged by distance and the fee is in your quote.

Will it be fresh?

Nothing sits on a shelf here. Your cake is baked for your date, and only a few orders are taken at a time so that stays true.
